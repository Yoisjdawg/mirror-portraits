**Put any picture on your portrait in World of Warcraft Forever:** a photo of you, your dog, or a screenshot from another game.

### What it does
- **Your portrait:** your picture replaces your WoW portrait on your player frame and your character sheet, and on your target frame when you target yourself. **Frame my portrait** lets you drag and zoom the round framing.
- **Your profile:** type **/mp** to open a panel with the whole picture, your name and a short title (like "the Good Boy").
- **Seen by other players:** anyone else with Mirror Portraits sees your name and title in your tooltip (through your guild, your group, or when they mouse over you). Only the text is shared; your picture stays on your screen.

### Adding your picture (this download)
1. Save your picture as a **512 x 512 TGA** file named **Portrait.tga** (Paint.NET, GIMP and Photoshop can all save TGA).
2. Put it in `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\MirrorPortraits`, next to `MirrorPortraits.toc`.
3. Restart WoW. WoW only finds new files when it starts.

**Want it to take any photo by itself?** The Melty version (https://melty.gg/m/mirror-portraits) includes a small helper that turns any PNG, JPG, BMP or GIF you drop into `Pictures\Mirror Portraits` into your portrait (even while you play), and starts WoW Forever through Battle.net in one click. CurseForge doesn't allow programs in add-on downloads, so that part is only on Melty.

### Commands
`/mp` open or close your profile · `/mp name <name>` · `/mp title <text>` · `/mp portrait on|off` · `/mp folder` · `/mp refresh` · `/mp reset` (`/mirror` works too)
